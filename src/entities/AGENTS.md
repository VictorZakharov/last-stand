# `src/entities`

The things that move and fight.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md); each directory under this one has an AGENTS.md of its own.

- `player.ts`: the player character: input to movement and skills, resources, damage, block, animation, and the remote players' copies in co-op. `movement.ts`: how a hero's body moves over the ground, by pushing against it with its feet.
- `enemy.ts`: an enemy: movement, status effects, actions, damage, death (its decisions are `enemyAI.ts`, its look `models/`). `enemyAI.ts`: the behaviours (each sets a velocity and a facing and starts timed actions; what an attack looks like goes through `fx`, which co-op guests replay). `spawner.ts`: a foe spawned with its summoning effect.
- `evade.ts`: a foe getting out of a hero's attack it sees coming (`EnemyDef.evade`): ducked into the ground or blinked to a spot nearby, at random (`chance`), rolled once an attack (`evades`, called by the skills as their attack reaches a foe).
- `carry.ts`: the foes a hero's charge sweeps up (Bull Rush): carried before him, thrown on ahead as it stops, and the fallen in its way driven on before it (`src/combat/skills/AGENTS.md`).
- `corpse.ts`: a fallen foe's body, from its fall until it has dissolved: its ragdoll thrown by the kill (`combat/fling.ts`), lying still a moment (`LIE`) and then dissolving, at the latest `LONGEST` after the fall or after a storm last held it. Frozen, it falls stiff and shatters where it lands; shocked, it twitches and sparks; burnt, it chars (`uChar`, `core/materials.ts`) and smokes. Skills move the fallen through it: a field while it lasts (`addField`: a storm's whirl, a beam's carry) and a blast or a shove (`blastCorpses`, its `once` the bodies already moved). `ragdoll.ts`: the body gone limp, 16 verlet points on the shared rig's joints. They are joined by its bones (held at their length), braces (within ranges: the trunk's bend and twist, the head on its neck) and the limbs' folds, with the knees kept apart and bending forward only. The points meet the floor, the props and the arena's wall, and the rig is posed from them every frame.
- `viewModel.ts`: first person, the hero's own arms and weapon in view (the Views gotcha in `src/core/AGENTS.md`), and a placement's fade there (`ViewFade`). `viewSwing.ts`: every blow through the eyes, and a two-hander's guard, swung in a plane, as a body swings one (`ViewSwing`, `swingAt`), and the key that holds a pose chosen in the view (`swingKeyFor`, the lab's `swing`). (Moves keyed by the grip's place and the weapon's way, `viewMove`, led with the flat and flipped the forearm, and are gone: #252.)

Gear-driven skills and block (`Player.gear`, `tryBlock`) are in `src/combat/skills/AGENTS.md`; how foes find their way round props is `src/world/AGENTS.md`.

## Gotchas

- **Hostile bolts leave the caster's tip** (`fireBolt` in `enemyAI.ts` sends `model.tip`'s height with the `bolt` fx), then settle to the level `BOLT_Y` over their first 2.2m: hits and the clear-shot check (`lineClear`) assume that level. A caster's cast pose should still hold its tip near bolt height (the Sporecaller holds its pod out at the target), so the settling is only a few centimetres.
- **An enemy's blow lands when its animation says so:** the data's `windup` is the one clock. The AI fires the strike, slam or shot at `windup / (windup + recover)` of the action, and `Enemy.animate` hands the same share to the model (`ActionState.hit`, from the data, so a co-op guest's copy has it too). A model's `attack` / `slam` brings the blow down to connect *at* `hit` (raise until just before it, strike ending on it, recover after) and a caster's build-up peaks on it, so a new enemy's animation is written against `a.hit`, never against fixed shares of the action. The dodge window is unchanged: `meleeStrike` still checks range and facing when it fires.
- **The shooters get out of the heroes' attacks** (`evade.ts`, `EnemyDef.evade`; the owner's words: the mushroom shooters "should duck down to a mushroom with a 50% chance, so the shots will miss, unless fired under their feet", the wraiths "teleport to a near location with 50% chance, escaping the shot", "from all ranged attack, including warrior crescent wave, and against bull rush too", and "only if player is in front of them... if another player shoots from behind, they cannot see that"). Each attack a foe sees is rolled once: a shot as it comes within `SEEN_AHEAD` (0.25 s) of the foe's body (`Projectile.foresee`), a cast's shots as one (`ProjectileOpts.judged`: judged arrow by arrow, a foe got out of every five-arrow volley), a wave, a charge or a beam as it reaches the foe, an area coming down as it first would strike it (Starfall's whole cast's, Hailfletch's channel's). It sees only an attack from a hero within `sees` (45°) either side of its facing, and only up and about (not rising, dazed, frozen, carried or already evading). The Sporecaller ducks (`EVADE_HOW.duck`) from shots alone: for `down` s it thinks of nothing, and its body sinks into the ground but for its cap (`Enemy.sunk`, drawn by its crowd's matrices: no mesh is hidden), over `DUCK.down`. As hits have it, it's down from the moment it ducks (`duckedTop`: a Lancewood Shot crosses 7 m in five frames, and met the body half down), struck only within its circle under its `top` (0.5 m, `strikesBody`'s `strikesDucked`): its skinned body is met by its hulls, which leave the cap's top out, and an arrow at the cap flew through. A shot aimed at its feet strikes it; one at a man's chest, a seeking bolt and Void Lance's beam pass over. The Rift Witch blinks (`EVADE_HOW.blink`) from every one of a hero's ranged attacks: to a spot `reach` (3 to 5.5 m) off it it could walk to in a line, clear of the props, the wall, every hero and the attack (`blinkSpot`, `inThreat`: off a line's sides or behind its front, out of an area's circle), torn out of the air where it stood and formed again there (`uDissolve` run back over `BLINK_FORM`, per foe in its crowd). A cast's other shots pass it by. Co-op: `evade.ts`'s header and `src/net/AGENTS.md`. `npm run lab -- evade` (`--class ranger`, `mage`, `warrior`) tries every attack at each from in front, behind and at a ducked foe's feet; `coop --scenarios evade` both screens.
- **A body is measured as drawn:** `npm run lab -- death` follows each skill's kills: every part of the drawn pose against the floor, the props and its points, its bones' stretch, the way it went. `npm run lab -- crowd` says what a crowd of bodies costs the frame, the CPU slowed as a low-end device's. Run both after a change to the ragdoll or to a skill's blow.
- **The trunk bends where the rig bends it, at the spine's joint.** The pelvis is the spine's point and the thighs', held at their lengths, so its turn is that triangle's and the drawn hips land on their points. With a point at the hips' joint, 2 cm over the thighs', the pelvis rolled round its thighs as the trunk bent, and a body folded at the waist lay 5 cm into the floor.
- **In verlet, a push is a speed.** A point moved without where it was a step ago gets the move as a speed: the knee hinge's fix rocked a knee a centimetre a frame for good, until it moved both (`shift`). The floor and the props' sides set where it was too, so a point leaves them in a small bounce and is slowed along them by their grip. Pushed out of a rock's side alone, a body leaning on it buzzed there and slid down it, and never lay still.
- **A charge's foes are placed by it alone** (`carry.ts`, `Enemy.carry` and `toss`): carried, a foe thinks of nothing and rides before its hero (`placeBefore`), the ones it carries stacked along its way, each behind another across its line kept behind it (`stackBefore`, in `separateEnemies`: pushed apart instead, one carried into the next was shoved out sideways 60 to 70 cm in a frame); thrown, it flies over the others and the players (`separateEnemies` skips it), tipped back as it goes (`tipOf`). It rides until the charge stops as its game plays it (`carrying`: a partner's copy's dash too, which runs a little behind his own game's), by the clock where no dash is seen. A co-op guest's copy its own hero carries is placed by its own game as its screen shows the hit (`placedOwn`): carried, pushed off every other copy as the host pushes the foe (`separateEnemies(true)`, its own copies alone), thrown on as the host throws it, the host's own `toss` of it then passed over, held where it came down until the host's reports have it within `HAND_OFF.near`, and eased onto them from there (`handOff`). One the host throws rises and falls as the `toss` fx tells every game, carried along the ground by the reports. `npm run lab -- coop --scenarios rush --class warrior` judges both screens.
- **A blow off the body's middle turns it** (`Ragdoll.strike`). The throw is shared out by height (struck high, a body topples over its feet), and a blow landing across the body from its middle (`Fling.ox`, `oz`) also turns it about its upright. The turn is a share (`TWIST`) of what the blow's lever over the body's own inertia would give it held rigid, and the part struck takes more of the throw (`KICK`), its momentum kept. Over its first 0.2 s, a cleave's kill turns about 13°, a charge's about 40°, a grazing arrow's up to 53° (`lab death`'s "turned"). A blast or a straight push lands through the middle and turns nothing.
- **Still means lying still.** A body sleeps once no point has moved faster than `STILL` from frame to frame for `STILL_FOR`, and never while a field holds it: hovering in a storm's eye, a body slept 0.8 m up. Asleep, it isn't stepped or posed until a field reaches it. Its limbs go limp (`LIMP`) once its trunk lies on something: with the air alone, an arm hanging from a shoulder on a rock swung for five seconds.
- **What a body costs:** a moving one takes 0.05 to 0.08 ms of a frame on a desktop, 0.4 to 0.7 ms with the CPU six times slower (`lab crowd`). A crowd of 38 killed at once takes 1.8 to 2.5 ms of a frame at its 90th percentile on the desktop (10 to 16 ms slowed), and the frame as it falls is no slower than with the crowd standing. Fewer passes in the air (`FLYING_PASSES`) and no pose while asleep made it cheaper. Fewer passes for a crowd (2 or 4) saved 3% of the frame, but stretched a landing's shins up to 37%, so they were taken out. With the foes drawn in crowds (`models/crowd.ts`, #222) the frame with 38 standing is 6 to 9 ms, so the bodies' peak is now a third of the frame as they fall: the ragdoll's solver (4 steps of 6 passes a frame at 60 a second) is what's left to make cheaper, and `lab crowd`'s budget for the bodies (20% of the frame standing) fails until it is.
- **What a dash and a cast let be cast** (`Player.tryCast`, `handleInput`, `holdAim`): nothing as a dash runs and holds unless it's open (`DashOpts.open`, Bull Rush's), when every skill but a charge or a blink is (`SkillBase.dashes`), another skill's dash riding on it as it runs (`DashState.ride`: posed for its time, its way given up, and nothing cast as it rides); nothing over a cast or a channel but a skill made to be (`SkillDef.overCast`, Bull Rush), which leaves it going. A cast or a channel as a dash runs keeps the body on the dash's way (`dashRunning`), and is the action shown, the dash under it (`ActionState.under`: `src/combat/skills/AGENTS.md`).
- **The hotbar shows what an action holds up** (`Player.heldUp`, the slot's `busy`, the touch buttons too): while a free-move cast (the healing draught) is under way every other skill, and a consumable while any other action is (a cast, a channel, a dash, a stagger), until the hero has been free for `BUSY_HOLD`: a held attack is free for a frame between its blows and its key, tried before the draught's, takes that frame, so shown free then the draught's slot flashed with every blow. Not every slot while anything is cast: an attack held down would dim the whole bar.
