# `src/combat`

What hits what.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md); each directory under this one has an AGENTS.md of its own.

- `damage.ts`: damage rolls and how they land, both ways: `hitEnemy` (a player's hit on a foe: his stats, the kill, leech, a mark's extra share), `hurtPlayer` (a foe's on the local hero), `dazeEnemy`, and the damage numbers. Each hit says where it's from for the battle log (`ui/battleLog.ts`): a hit on a foe its skill (`HitOpts.skill`, every skill's hits pass their own `def`), a blow on the hero the foe and its attack (`Attacker`: a strike, a slam, a bolt). They go to the log through its sink, null while the log is off.
- `fling.ts`: what a killing blow does to the body it kills (`entities/corpse.ts`). The body is thrown the way the blow drove it, as hard as the damage past the life it had left (`FLING` in `data/balance.ts`), less for a foe that stands its ground or a boss. It falls by what killed it: frozen, shocked or burnt. A skill says how its blow drives (`HitOpts.blow`: its way, lift, force, where up the body it lands, and `on`, where across it: off its middle it turns the body); `swingWay` is a sweep's way along its swing, `swingOn` where its blade meets the body. The killer's game works the fling out and co-op carries it (the `die` event's `f`, a guest's hit report), so every game throws the body alike.
- `projectiles.ts`: every projectile, the players' and the foes': its flight, its world collision (the props, the arena's wall) and its hit. A frame's step strikes the foes it meets in the order it meets them, the first alone unless it pierces. A level shot (an arrow, `level`) strikes a foe where it meets its body as drawn (`strikesBody` in `strikes.ts`: its rigid meshes and its skinned limbs, as the aim meets them), with its point, half its length ahead (`point`), and before its own flight puts it into the ground, so an arrow aimed low at a foe strikes it, not the floor in front; any other shot (a seeking bolt) strikes within the foe's circle at any height, or its body where that reaches past it. Struck only within its circle and under its height, an arrow aimed at a boss's crown, arms or chest flew on through, and one at a boss's legs struck the imp whose circle it passed. The bow's preview (`flightPath` in `skills/ranger.ts`) ends where the shot would strike, by the same test. Measure with `npm run lab -- shots --class ranger` (`--ab`: `main` fails most of its shots).
- `lures.ts`: something standing in the arena that draws foes off the players for a while (the ranger's Strawman, `skills/AGENTS.md`).
- `skills/`: the skills' behaviours.

In co-op a hit counts only where `net/role.ts` says (the root's Co-op rule), so damage stays in `hitEnemy` and hits on players in `hurtPlayer`. Hostile bolts and an enemy's blows are the enemies' (`src/entities/AGENTS.md`).
