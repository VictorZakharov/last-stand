# `src/combat/skills`

Each skill's behaviour, registered by its `impl` name in `index.ts` (`SKILL_IMPLS`) and referenced from class data. `types.ts` is the contract: an instant skill has `cast()`, a channel `start/tick/stop` with a typed state object, and `Needs<'field'>` requires optional `SkillDef` tuning fields.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md).

- **The warrior:** `cleave.ts` (Rending Cleave), `crescent.ts` (Thunder Crescent), `powerStrike.ts`, `twinFangs.ts`, `bullRush.ts`, `raiseShield.ts`, `tempest.ts` (Steel Tempest), `ironBellow.ts`; `slash.ts` draws the swept blade arcs they share.
- **The mage:** `splinterBolt.ts`, `voidLance.ts`, `glacialNova.ts`, `starfall.ts`, `maelstrom.ts` (its look is `fx/firestorm.ts`), `blink.ts` (Veilstep).
- **The ranger:** `ranger.ts` (the bow's shots and arrows), `arrowRain.ts` (Hailfletch), `quarryMark.ts`, `strawman.ts`.
- **Shared:** `potion.ts` (the healing draught), `readiness.ts` (a held skill's preview while a release would cast nothing).

A skill runs on every game for its visuals: its damage goes through `hitEnemy` and its hits on players through `hurtPlayer` (the root's Co-op rule). A skill with materials of its own returns sample meshes from its `warm()` hook (the root's shader rule). An area skill calls `hurtPropsIn` next to its `hitEnemy` loop (Breakable props in `src/world/AGENTS.md`). Its effect is held to the light budget: its materials wrapped in `nearGlow()`, and measured with `npm run lab -- cast <skill>` (Bright effects in `src/core/AGENTS.md`). A held skill gives its preview a `Readiness` and a reader in the lab's `held`. Its icon: `src/ui/skillArt/AGENTS.md`.

## Gotchas

- **Killing blows drive the bodies** (`combat/fling.ts`, `entities/corpse.ts`). Each hit says how its blow drives what it kills (`blow`):
  - a sweep along its swing (`swingWay`, the way its blade trail shows it: `model.swing`);
  - an arrow on along its flight (`arrowBlow`);
  - a blast out and up from where it struck;
  - a slam down into the floor (Power Strike).

  A blow that knows where across the body it struck says so (`on`), and off the body's middle it turns the body as it throws it: an arrow or a bolt where it hit, Void Lance's beam where it passes the body, Bull Rush on the side nearest the charge's line, a sweep's blade on the body's near side (`swingOn`: pushed there along the swing, the body turns with its front carried on along the blade's way). A blast or a chop strikes through the middle and turns nothing.

  The bodies already lying are moved too. A blast throws them (`blastCorpses`), each once a cast where a cast strikes again and again (`once`: Starfall's crystals each threw the same bodies, and one flew 17 m; Bull Rush's plough; Thunder Crescent's wave); a body the same blow killed is thrown by its own fling. A channel or a storm that holds the fallen adds a field while it lasts (`addField`, taken away in `stop`): Void Lance carries them along its beam, and Maelstrom whirls them round it and burns them. `npm run lab -- death` follows each skill's kills.
- **Cooldowns are per skill id**, shared by every key the skill is bound to (the same spell may sit on several keys).
- **Gear-driven skills and block:** `Player.gear` comes from the equipped items: weapon base, two-handed (`ClassDef.twoHanded`), shield (an off-hand with Block Amount), and a weapon in the off-hand (`ClassDef.dualWield`: a one-hander fits the off-hand slot, and its implicit counts at `OFFHAND_WEAPON`). Each weapon style (shield, two-handed, dual, one-handed) has its own loadout (`loot/loadout.ts` `weaponStyle`); changing style loads that style's, and a style not set up yet starts from the class defaults fitted to the gear. With two weapons the model's swings alternate hands, and `tip` moves to the swinging weapon. A two-hander is held in both hands, the left one following its lower grip by arm IK (`fistReach` in `models/hands.ts`); a pose that takes that grip out of the left arm's reach (the war cry's flung arms; Steel Tempest held it in one hand until it was placed by the weapon, `docs/agents/heroes.md`) lets go with it, the arm easing back to the pose's own, rather than leaving the hand floating off the wrist. `Model.setGear` shows it, and `profile.equipFromStash` never lets a two-hander and an off-hand be equipped together (an off-hand equipped puts the two-hander back in the stash); the lobby shows a two-hander's empty off-hand locked, a padlock whose tooltip names the weapon taking both hands and, for a class with off-hands, says what equipping one does. A skill with `needs: 'shield'` (or `'twoHanded'`) gives way to its `fallback` for the gear held (`Player.skillAt`), and the lobby dims it in the skill book while the gear doesn't allow it (`Player.usable`). Blocking is in `Player.tryBlock`: a per-hit chance with a recovery (`BLOCK` in `data/balance.ts`), or every frontal hit while a skill with `block` is channelled, until a hit bigger than that breaks the guard and staggers the player (`guardBroken`, `staggered`: move only). Melee skills draw their trail through the weapon tip (`skills/cleave.ts` `swingArc`), so keep the model's swing timing in step with it; the weapon itself leaves a white trail wherever it's swung (`fx/swingTrail.ts`, `Model.swung`: a new strike's action is one of the warrior model's `STRIKES`), which needs nothing of a skill. A skill's `styles` tunes it for a weapon style over its own (`loot/loadout.ts` `forStyle`, applied by `Player.fitToGear` as the gear changes, which the lab's `--gear` calls too: set by the gear alone, a two-hander swung at a one-hander's pace). Rending Cleave swings slower and harder with a two-hander (0.6 s, 92) and quicker and lighter with two blades (0.3 s, 45), each about as much damage and energy a second as with one, and Steel Tempest spins slower with one (`spin`, 10.5 rad/s against 15: it strikes once each 3 rad round, so each strike is harder, the same damage a second); the HUD's and the lobby's tooltips show the skill as it is with the weapons held.
- **Thunder Crescent is driven into the ground** (`crescent.ts`, the warrior's `smite`, `docs/agents/heroes.md`): a two-hander raised overhead and brought down onto the floor ahead at the skill's `fireAt`, its tip on it whatever the weapon's length. The crescent is cast as the blade bites: a flash, a ring and cracks where the tip is (2 m ahead through the eyes, where the weapon is placed in the view instead), and it runs low along the ground from 0.6 m ahead, as far as it did, so foes close in front are still struck. 280 damage, every 4 s.
- **Drawn shots** (`SkillDef.draw`, the Ranger's mouse skills and Lancewood Shot): drawn while the key is held (`draw` s to full), loosed when it's let go but never before `minDraw` of that; `castTime` is then what follows the release (the follow-through and the next arrow). `Player.tryCast` starts one with no end, `loose` gives it one, and `fire` passes how far it was drawn (`power`) to the skill's `cast`; the pose reads `ActionState.draw`, `loosed`, `pitch` (the skill's `pitch` hook: the arc for the arrow as it would leave), `yaw`, `arrows` and `fan`. A drawn shot pressed for while the hero is busy (a tap during the last one's reload) is queued and drawn as soon as he's free, in the frame the last one ends (started the next frame, the pose had a frame with no shot between them and the bow stepped out to rest and back), loosed at once if its key is up again: tapping fires least-draw shots one after another. A bow's hero (`ClassDef.quiver`) keeps an arrow on the string between shots (`Player.nocked`: on once taken from the quiver, gone with the shot, back on as the cast's reload ends, so a cut reload leaves none) and puts it back after `idle` s without a shot; a bow's shot with none on the string takes one from the quiver first (`CastState.nockT`, `fetch` s, before the draw's time and the least draw count; the model gets `ActionState.fetch`), and co-op's `cast` carries it (`n`). Pressing or casting any other skill forgets it. Touch holds a drawn bow while the button is held and looses as it's let go. Co-op: the `cast` action carries `h` (held: `dur` is the time to full draw) and `fire` carries `k`, the draw. Arrows (`skills/ranger.ts`) fly as fast as the draw sent them (a bow drives its arrow about in proportion to the pull, `pullAt`), hit as hard as the energy that gives (the pull's square), on the lower ballistic arc that crosses the target at a man's chest (`launchPitch`), turning to follow their path; they pass over a foe they're above (`ProjectileOpts.level`). A projectile's `tick` may end its flight. A drawn shot's `charging` hook runs every frame of its draw with how far it's drawn: every bow's shot glints once it's drawn to full (`drawGlint`: motes of light drawn in onto the arrowhead until it's loosed, with the bow's ready sound as it comes to full, and nothing as it's drawn, so the light says it's full: a burst alone and nothing after read as the charge gone; `fx/glintMotes.ts`, specks a few pixels across drawn by one quad on the arrowhead, as glow particles fade within 3.5 m of the camera and through the eyes nothing of them showed; a star of light there read as a bright spark, too bright in every view), and in the close views the crosshair is bold (`Player.drawnFull`, `#crosshair.full`: swelling into it once as a heart knocks, about its own middle, never pulsing on, none with reduced motion; on a layer of its own, the swell the compositor's; centred by `transform`, it swelled about where its box was before that and shifted up and left, so it's centred by `translate`), the hit hardest at full draw (from `FULL_DRAW` of the draw, Lancewood Shot's harder still, its `fullBonus`). Measure with `npm run lab -- draw --class ranger` (`--canary`). While the local hero draws one, a thin red line previews each arrow's path if it were loosed now (`fx/aimLaser.ts`, from `flightPath` in `skills/ranger.ts`: the arrow's own flight stepped as the projectile steps it, a frame at 60 fps, moving and then falling, with its collisions: the ground, the arena's edge, a prop it doesn't clear, the first foe unless it pierces, over a foe it's above by its height at the step's end; stepped otherwise, a grazing arrow's hit came out the other way), with a dot where it strikes: a fixed few pixels wide at any distance (widened in screen space in its vertex shader, so not `nearGlow`-dimmed: it never fills the view), faded near the camera and eased in and out (never flashing), only on its own player's screen. Measure it against the shots (a fixed-clock probe: the preview's ends read from its meshes in the frame the shot is loosed, against where each arrow ended, every skill, draw, target and view: within a frame's flight) and the share of the first-person view's middle it covers. Lancewood Shot goes on through every foe in its line (`ProjectileOpts.pierce`: each one met in a frame in the order it passes them, each taking its `falloff` share in turn) and hits short ones too (not `level`: flying near level it passed over the nearest heads); a fast arrow is gone in two frames, so it leaves a streak of light from the bow that fades (`streakGeo`).
  - **Spent arrows stay** (`fx/stuckArrows.ts`): `src/fx/AGENTS.md`.
- **The ranger's other skills** (`skills/arrowRain.ts`, `quarryMark.ts`, `strawman.ts`; a mark on the floor lies on it everywhere, `fx/floorPatch.ts`: cut along the dais's edges, each piece flat on its level, and down the face of each step it crosses; in one plane at its middle's height, the rain's area went under the dais where it crossed the step, and laid by the ground under each point, a triangle across the step sloped between levels and notched the dais's edge):
  - **Hailfletch** is a bow's channel: standing still (`moveMult` 0), volley after volley over the area, each posed as a shot (`ChannelSkill.pose` fills in the action's draw, loose, fetch and aim as a cast's would; the model takes the next volley's string where its reload left the hand, `kHook`), drawn near level and lifted to its angle as the string comes back (raised first, the draw shoulder went past its range as the hand took the string: steep shots aren't what the bow pose was tuned for). Each volley's arrow splits over the area into a shower fanning out from that point and coming down together on spots anywhere in the area, each arrow striking the foe its point comes down through (`struck`, `damage` an arrow: a foe may take several or none), before the next arrow goes up (spread over the next volley's time, the sheaf was a steady drizzle that read as nothing to do with the shots); what's in the air when the key is let go still comes down. Arrows are a few pixels from the top-down view: the falling ones carry streaks of light, or the rain reads as nothing.
  - **Aimed while held** (`SkillDef.aimed`: Quarry Mark and Strawman, and the mage's Starfall and Veilstep): the key held, the skill's `aim` hook shows where it would land on its own player's screen alone (the foe it would mark outlined in red, `ui/overlay/outline.ts`: the reticle over its head was at times hard to make out; the scarecrow's glowing outline with a ring at its foot and the ring it calls foes from; the area Starfall's first crystal strikes, landing on the aim: shown round all its crystals may strike, it took in foes none struck), and let go it is cast where the aim was as it was let go, waiting for an action under way as a queued shot does (cast at the aim as the cast fired, a fraction of a second on, it went to what the mouse had moved onto by then: Quarry Mark's outline round the boss, its mark on a small foe); its slot shows active meanwhile. Every preview is red while letting go would cast nothing (on its cooldown, short of energy), with the seconds of the cooldown counting down in it, and turns to its own look once it would (`combat/skills/readiness.ts`; Quarry Mark's outline, red already, dims instead), so it can be held through the cooldown and let go once ready. A new held skill gives its preview a `Readiness` and a reader in the lab's `held` (`READERS`). Measure with `npm run lab -- held --class ranger` (and `mage`). The preview is shown once the foes have moved (`Player.showAim` from `main`'s update): shown in the hero's own update, before theirs, it trailed its foe by the frame's move. On touch a button held is its key held (`ui/touch.ts`): the same preview while the finger is down, aimed where the finger drags to or where a tap aims, and cast as it lifts, where the preview last showed; a cancelled touch gives the aim up (`Player.endAim`) rather than casting. (Cast on release with a plain ring for the drag, the touch screen never showed a skill's own preview.) Measure with `npm run lab -- tap --class mage` (and `ranger`).
  - **Lures** (`combat/lures.ts`): Strawman is a lure, and where the fight is simulated a foe it draws (`lurable`: not elites, bosses, dummies or a guest's copies) goes for it instead of its target (`Enemy.lure`, enemyAI's `lured`: its own attack at it, a brute's slam with its ring, the rest up close, a caster too, which is what gathers them) along its own flow field (`LURE_SLOT` up). A blow on one shows on every game through enemyAI's `fx('lureHit')`, found by where it fell.
  - **Quarry Mark's reticle** stands over its quarry's crown (`Enemy.overHead`: the top of its opaque meshes as built, `Reach.crown`, or its height if that's more), as do a dazed foe's stars. Placed by its height, it stood inside a boss's crown, which reaches up to 1.8 times that, and the body hid it in every view. Measure with `npm run lab -- held --class ranger` (the reticle over each boss in each view, none of it inside a body, `page/markSeen.ts`).
  - **Quarry Mark's quarry** is the foe whose body the aim's ray met (`input.foe`, kept as `Player.aimFoe`), the one the outline is round; with none (touch, or the aim on the floor) the nearest the aim (`offBody`: its upright cylinder, how deep in it the aim is). Let go, the cast is aimed at the quarry's middle (the skill's `settle` hook gives the point a cast is aimed at), and every game picks the foe from that point, which each is given, so each marks the same one. Picked from the aim's point on the body, a small foe pressed against a boss took the mark the outline showed round the boss; judged by their middles, a boss lost to a small foe beside it, the aim on its body a metre or two from its middle.
  - **Marks** (`Enemy.marks`): a marked foe takes the largest mark's extra share from every player's hits (`hitEnemy`). Every game marks its own copy of the foe as it sees the cast land (each game's counts for its own hero's hits), and each runs the cooldowns' refund from the foe's `onDeath`, which a guest's copy runs too; only the local hero's cooldowns are refunded. A kill spends the mark.
  - An instant skill with nothing to cast at says so and costs nothing (`InstantSkill.canCast`).
- **Veilstep, the mage's blink** (`skills/blink.ts`): aimed while held (a ring, a disc and a column of light where he would come out: towards the aim, within his reach and the arena, back along the way to the first spot no prop stands on; red and counting down while letting go would cast nothing, as every held skill's preview is), and let go he comes apart where he stands (`uDissolve`, `VANISH`), crosses as a streak of light (a dash with `through`: the props aren't pushed against until it ends; `guardUntil`: nothing strikes him from coming apart until he forms again; his model reset every frame he is gone, or his planted feet and his sleeves' cloth stretched back after him) and forms again where he was aimed. The foes within `radius` of where he vanished are dazed (`Enemy.daze`: for `duration` s, less for those resisting freezing; a dazed foe drops what it was doing, a slam's telegraph with it, thinks of nothing and has stars circling over its head, `fx/dazed.ts`). A daze is a gameplay effect and is judged as a hit is: by the caster's own game alone (`dazeEnemy` in `combat/damage.ts`), a guest's riding its hit reports to the host (`HitReport`'s last number) and the host's snapshots flagging every dazed foe (`4`) for the other games; a foe a guest shows dazed lands no blow on it (`FX.melee`). A partner's copy goes the blink's own way as its game moves it and is eased onto its reports as it forms: read between them, it set off before it had come apart and overshot by metres as it crossed. Measure with `npm run lab -- blink --class mage` and `coop --class mage --scenarios blink` (at 300 ms, a copy comes out within a centimetre of where its hero did, and a foe dazed on the guest's screen walks on a median of half a metre there before the host's daze reaches it).
