# `src/fx`

What lights up and flies about.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

- `particles.ts`: CPU-simulated, GPU-drawn point particles, an additive pool (glows, sparks) and an alpha-blended one (smoke, dust, debris). `lights.ts`: the fixed pool of 8 point lights (the root's rule: never a `PointLight` of your own). `effects.ts`: transient effects (shockwaves, decals, telegraphs, lightning, ice), each `{ update(dt) -> alive, dispose() }`.
- **Looks of their own:** `firestorm.ts` (Maelstrom), `riftBolts.ts` and `sporeOrb.ts` (the foes' bolts), `glintMotes.ts` (a drawn arrow at full), `aimLaser.ts` (a drawn shot's path), `floorPatch.ts` (a mark lying on the floor across the dais's steps), `dazed.ts` (a dazed foe's stars), `stuckArrows.ts` (spent arrows), `rubble.ts` and `rubbleGeo.ts` (a broken prop's pieces: Breakable props in `src/world/AGENTS.md`).

An effect's material is wrapped in `nearGlow()` and its light held to the budget (Bright effects in `src/core/AGENTS.md`); one that brings its own materials is warmed at boot (`game/warmup.ts`).

## Gotchas

- **Spent arrows stay** (`fx/stuckArrows.ts`) until there are `STUCK_MAX` (1000), and then the oldest fade out, shrinking into where they're buried: in the floor, a wooden prop, a glowcap or embers, the Thornwood's thicket (and down with a prop when it breaks), or a foe's body, riding the rigid piece it struck until the body dissolves, when they drop and lie on the floor. Off stone (the dais, the crypt's floor and wall, stone props: `BiomeDef.surface`, a prop's rubble `kind`) they glance, tumble and lie flat, keeping a share of their speed that grows with it (`GLANCE`: a slow arrow hardly moves, a full draw's skids a metre or two and hops at most 20 cm; a fixed share sent it skidding metres at any speed). A prop is gone into where it draws its surface (`Prop.raycast`, each look's: an instance alone, its meshes; `propSurface`), not where its circle has it: on the circle an arrow went most of its length into a log or hung half a metre off a glowcap; where the line meets nothing it draws (past a log's end, between a cluster's caps) the arrow flies on past that prop (`onProp` returns false, `Projectile.passed`; dropped there, it lay on the floor short of the log). The aim is what's under the cursor or the crosshair as it's drawn, nothing assumed (`aimAlong` in `core/input.ts`: the floor, a foe's rigid meshes, a prop's own, nearest along the ray, each tried only once its cylinder is entered; the crosshair with nothing within aim range, the point that far along it), its `y` how high over the floor under it, and a bow's arc goes through that point (`launchPitch`: aimed at a man's chest over the floor, a log was shot over, a shot at the floor flew on, and on a foe the aim snapped to its feet), no steeper than `MAX_PITCH` and no further down than `MIN_PITCH`. Co-op's `fire` carries the height as `y`. Nothing else reads the aim's height; a probe that draws nothing must update the scene's matrices itself, or the foes' meshes are met where they spawned. Measure each wooden prop's buried length by a ray from the nock along the arrow, from every side and height. Shots meet the wall itself (`world/edge.ts`: the crypt's octagon, the ring of thicket, a gate's opening the way out; `ProjectileOpts.edge`, `onProp`), not the 28 m circle a bolt fades at. A body is found by a ray against its rigid meshes only (0.1 ms; against a creature's skinned meshes a ray cost 1 to 9 ms, every vertex moved by its bones first). They're drawn as two instanced meshes: the whole arrow (301 triangles) within 6 m of the camera, `arrowLite` (18) beyond: a thousand whole ones were 300k triangles and 3 ms of GPU. Measure their cost as the same frames with them shown and hidden in turn (the scene's own load drifts as a wave goes on: a thousand cost nothing measurable, their update 0.01 to 0.06 ms a frame with a third riding foes), and where they lie against the floor, the props and the wall, shot for real at each surface in both biomes.
