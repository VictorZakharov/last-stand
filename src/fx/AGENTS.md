# `src/fx`

What lights up and flies about.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

- `particles.ts`: CPU-simulated, GPU-drawn point particles, an additive pool (glows, sparks) and an alpha-blended one (smoke, dust, debris). `lights.ts`: the fixed pool of 8 point lights (the root's rule: never a `PointLight` of your own). `effects.ts`: transient effects (shockwaves, decals, telegraphs, lightning, ice), each `{ update(dt) -> alive, dispose() }`.
- **Looks of their own:** `firestorm.ts` (Maelstrom), `riftBolts.ts` and `sporeOrb.ts` (the foes' bolts), `glintMotes.ts` (a drawn arrow at full), `aimLaser.ts` (a drawn shot's path), `floorPatch.ts` (a mark lying on the floor across the dais's steps), `dazed.ts` (a dazed foe's stars: three plain stars floating round over its head, drawn as they are in one draw for every dazed foe, not as lights: four glows each leaving a trail made a bright ring over every dazed foe), `stuckArrows.ts` (spent arrows), `rubble.ts` and `rubbleGeo.ts` (a broken prop's pieces: Breakable props in `src/world/AGENTS.md`), `swingTrail.ts` (the white trail a swung weapon leaves), `vine.ts` (the Barkhide Hulk's vine and its coils round a held hero; from play: "vines need to be fancier and look like intertwining vines as they deploy". Three strands braided round its middle, its head reared off the floor, splayed and weaving as it creeps, leaves unfurling and tendrils curling off it once it has passed, the head settling once it's grown (`uHead`); the coils three strands erupting from under the floor and winding up his legs, crossing as they go. Every vertex knows how far along its piece it is, and swells from nothing as the growth passes it: one mesh a piece, opaque, lit by a light of its own from above, one program for every biome and quality, the sap in its grooves its only glow. Its leaves' colours are linear: written as sRGB they came out lime.) `effects.ts`'s `lavaPool` is the pool a Cinder Brute's chunk leaves (groundFlash's program, nothing new to compile).

An effect's material is wrapped in `nearGlow()` and its light held to the budget (Bright effects in `src/core/AGENTS.md`); one that brings its own materials is warmed at boot (`game/warmup.ts`).

## Gotchas

- **An instanced pool's new piece is placed as it's spawned** (`rubble.ts`): a slot's matrix is the pool's unset one until the next update sets it, the world's middle at full size, so a piece drawn first in between stood there for a frame (a broken prop's rubble, a pale rock a metre across on the dais beside the hero as Thunder Crescent broke the props ahead of him). Find such a pop with `lab cast`'s blinks and `--what`.
- **An empty pool is hidden** (`rubble.ts`, `particles.ts`, `dazed.ts`): three still sets up an empty mesh's program and uniforms each frame, though an instanced mesh of none never reaches the GPU, and the 23 rubble pools, frustum culling off, were 23 to 46 such draws a frame (`npm run lab -- draws` lists the draws of nothing). Each is shown through all of the boot's warm-ups, so every program compiles in each biome's light, and hidden by its own update alone (`updateRubble`, the pools' `update`), which runs only once the loop starts, from then on while it holds nothing. (Hidden by the warm-up's last `clearRubble`, the forest's wood rubble compiled mid-fight: the lobby's warm-up in each biome drew none of it, `npm run lab -- compiles`.) A new pool keeps both, and a name (`rubble: chip 3`, `particles: glow`): the lab's reports and the perf report name what drew by it.
- **A swung weapon's trail** (`swingTrail.ts`, updated in `main.ts` after `viewArms`): the band its striking part swept over the last tenth of a second, white, brightest along its edge's path, for every weapon a strike swings (`Model.swung`: the warrior's model lists his weapons while a strike's action and its follow-through play, each by two points of its striking part along its grip, `Weapon.edge` in `models/warrior.ts`). It's kept in the hero's own frame (his model's root's space), so through the eyes, where his arms are placed in the view and drawn by their own camera, it is drawn with them on their layer (`armsLayerOf` in `entities/viewModel.ts`) and lies where the weapon is seen (drawn by the world's camera, a narrower view's weapon and a wider one's trail parted). Each frame's place is a point of a centripetal Catmull-Rom curve through them, cut into 5 cm steps, so a blade crossing half a metre a frame stays round. A place is lit as fast as the slower of the moves into it and on from it (the newest by the move before), from 4 m/s to whole at 10: a wind-up and a walk's arm leave none, and a pose put elsewhere for a frame is no swing (through the eyes Steel Tempest's blade was put back 53 cm in a frame as it ended, and by its own move left a slash across the top of the view). Nothing is taken for a jump by its speed: through the eyes Thunder Crescent's blow crosses the view at 93 m/s. Its values are tunable (`swingTrail`: `--tune`). Measure with `npm run lab -- trail` (`--canary`), and its light with `lab cast <strike>`.
- **Particles are sized in the scene's pixels** (`pointScale()` in `core/renderer.ts`: the scene's target's height): sized by the canvas's height, they were as many times too wide as the canvas has more pixels than the scene, wherever the quality caps the scene's pixels: on #243's phone (a canvas of 3120x1440, the scene 1048x483) 3 times as wide, and Glacial Nova's filled 7.7M pixels a frame, 15 times the scene's (0.69M now). `npm run lab -- perf` says the particles' fill.
- **Spent arrows stay** (`fx/stuckArrows.ts`) until the arena shows another biome (`clearSpent`, on the `biomeChanged` event `world/arena.ts` sends: the floor and props they stood in aren't there) or there are `STUCK_MAX` (1000), and then the oldest fade out, shrinking into where they're buried: in the floor, a wooden prop (the small trees in the play area among them: `npm run lab -- trees --class ranger`), a glowcap or embers, the Thornwood's thicket (and down with a prop when it breaks), or a foe's body, riding the rigid piece it struck until the body dissolves, when they drop and lie on the floor. Off stone (the dais, the crypt's floor and wall, stone props: `BiomeDef.surface`, the wall's parts (`world/wall.ts`), a prop's rubble `kind`) they glance, tumble and lie flat, keeping a share of their speed that grows with it (`GLANCE`: a slow arrow hardly moves, a full draw's skids a metre or two and hops at most 20 cm; a fixed share sent it skidding metres at any speed). A prop is gone into where it draws its surface (`Prop.raycast`, each look's: an instance alone, its meshes; `propSurface`), not where its circle has it: on the circle an arrow went most of its length into a log or hung half a metre off a glowcap; where the line meets nothing it draws (past a log's end, between a cluster's caps) the arrow flies on past that prop (`onProp` returns false, `Projectile.passed`; dropped there, it lay on the floor short of the log). The aim is what's under the cursor or the crosshair as it's drawn, nothing assumed (`aimAlong` in `core/input.ts`: the floor, a foe's rigid meshes, a prop's own, nearest along the ray, each tried only once its cylinder is entered; the crosshair with nothing within aim range, the point that far along it), its `y` how high over the floor under it, and a bow's arc goes through that point (`launchPitch`: aimed at a man's chest over the floor, a log was shot over, a shot at the floor flew on, and on a foe the aim snapped to its feet), no steeper than `MAX_PITCH` and no further down than `MIN_PITCH`. Co-op's `fire` carries the height as `y`. Nothing else reads the aim's height; a probe that draws nothing must update the scene's matrices itself, or the foes' meshes are met where they spawned. Measure each wooden prop's buried length by a ray from the nock along the arrow, from every side and height. Arrows meet the wall where it's drawn (`world/wall.ts`: its stone, the thicket's bushes and rocks, a tree's trunk beyond it, each part stone or not), over it they fly on (into the trees, the ground, or out at `ARROW_EDGE`, past the furthest trees), and out through a gate under its arch they're gone (`throughGate`); the aim's preview (`flightPath`) meets them the same. With no height to the wall an arrow over the thicket stood in the air at its line (#290; `npm run lab -- beyond --class ranger`). An arrow falling in the arena is kept off the wall as drawn (`keepIn`), one that came over it falls where it is. Other shots meet `ProjectileOpts.edge`, the 28 m circle a bolt fades at. A body is found by a ray against its rigid meshes only (0.1 ms; against a creature's skinned meshes a ray cost 1 to 9 ms, every vertex moved by its bones first). They're drawn as two instanced meshes: the whole arrow (301 triangles) within 6 m of the camera, `arrowLite` (18) beyond: a thousand whole ones were 300k triangles and 3 ms of GPU. Measure their cost as the same frames with them shown and hidden in turn (the scene's own load drifts as a wave goes on: a thousand cost nothing measurable, their update 0.01 to 0.06 ms a frame with a third riding foes), and where they lie against the floor, the props and the wall, shot for real at each surface in both biomes.
