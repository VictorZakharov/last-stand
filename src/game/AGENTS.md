# `src/game`

The run and what goes round it.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

- `run.ts`: the run: its waves, scoring, loot rolls, and the choice after every wave to bank or go on (the core loop, the root's Product rules). In co-op the host's game runs it for the party and the guests follow its reports.
- `warmup.ts`: one of everything that brings its own materials made and drawn, so nothing compiles mid-fight (the root's shader rule). The lobby's before it shows: its biome compiled as it's built (`compileTheBiome`, a light standing in for the hero's), its effects, loot and the shown class's skills compiled with the hero as he's built (`compileTheLobby`: compiled with the lobby's warm-up, after the lobby's panels were painted, their links began 0.6 s after they were asked for), and all drawn by `warmLobby` with the hero wearing everything he can, the other biomes left out; another class's skills are warmed with its hero (`heroParts`; `npm run lab -- compiles --switch <class>`), on a first visit (which opens on the hero selection screen) with the lobby's own warm-up, their links waited for together (`warmLobby(true)`: warmed after it, in turn, the screen showed 0.8 s later still, 8.7 to 9.0 s into a first visit against 7.9 to 8.1; most of what's left is the two heroes' builds, their heads' sculpts 0.85 s of it); the rest after it (`warmTheRest`), as parts (a foe kind, another biome drawn in place of the lobby's, another class's hero with everything it can wear), built where the lobby's camera never looks and never let act. The fight's foes are built before the lobby shows, as its warm-up waits on the GPU (`buildAhead`: a kind's first build, its geometry sculpted and its crowd's merged, is one task of 30 to 90 ms, and built behind the lobby each held it up as long; a return's reveal came 0.2 to 0.4 s later), and kept out of the scene and their crowd till warmed. `warm.ts`: what's warm, and what waits for it (`whenWarm`): the fight's parts are warmed behind the lobby once the loading screen has left (its logo's exit draws over the lobby till then: `loadingLeft`), one at a time, a step a frame (`core/shaders.ts` `warmUp`'s `step`: a return's lobby kept 126 to 135 frames a second at the least, where with a part's steps back to back it drew 18 to 36), where they come from the GPU's cache; else at once once Start or a biome pick needs them, under the fight's loading bar (`ui/readying.ts`: about 1 s on a first visit, where the lobby's frames had linked the foes' programs one by one, waiting for each, for 5 s); the other heroes' (and the hall of heroes' room) only when Change hero, a save slot or co-op pick needs them (a hero's first build held the lobby up 250 ms: his head's hair). Warm is a build that warmed the fight on this machine before (`last-stand.fight-warmed` in local storage: the builds by their code's chunk, so a deploy that changed a shader is new) with its maps kept: kept maps alone said nothing of the programs, and a return after a visit that never fought compiled the fight cold behind the lobby, 0 to 6 frames a second for 3 s (`npm run stalls -- --scenario boot --cold-return`). A part with a program not linked 150 ms after its commands were done is cold too, given up before its draw. Each part is timed as a user timing measure, `warm <part>` (the stalls' boot lists the longest steps). The load is marked as the lobby is lit (`markLoaded` in `main.ts`): a program a warm-up makes after it is the boot's (`inTurn`), any other counts in the perf report as compiled late (marked once the fight's were warm, a new build's lobby counted every program late: `npm run lab -- programs --cold`).
- `hall.ts`: the hall of heroes, the hero selection screen's stage (`ui/heroSelect.ts`): every class's hero a remote player's copy (its casts for show alone, dealing nothing) on a plinth in the hall's room (`world/heroHall.ts`, shown in the biome's place: `G.arena.showHall`), the lobby's hero hidden meanwhile; the camera's shot on the one in focus (`stageCamera` in `core/renderer.ts`, gliding back to the lobby's view as it closes; at a man's eye height and over, aimed at his chest, "not groin area", and on an upright screen at his middle, `SHOT`: at his chest there the panel stood over his legs), a key and a rim light held from the pool (`hold: Infinity`, never stolen by a flash; the key a metre and a half before him with a short reach, `KEY`, as the heroes stand 1.75 m apart and a far one lit his neighbours almost as much, the room's own light low, its braziers at a third of the crypt's and its iron rougher (at half, with the crypt's polished iron, their bowls' rims took the fire's light as a white spot, "reflections are glaring"), and his highlights capped, `Player.shine`: from play, "spotlight should be brighter, and off spotlight should be dimmer", "make reflections less bright"; no spot light, which would change every lit program; a glowing weapon head lit by its own light, its faces under the bloom's threshold with a halo and a hot core of its own (`HEAD`, `HALO`, `CORE`): the mage's raised crystal came within 0.86 m of the key light, which lit its polished facets white and flared as each turned to it, "the staff's light is too bright when its flickering", and lit less it was a flat stone between flares, "most of the time its completely boring"; its glow is held clear of the bloom's all-or-nothing threshold, or its halo comes and goes, `npm run lab -- hall --glow`), his showcase cast as it arrives (a channel held for its `hold` and let go: the mage's Void Lance, its portal beside him and its beam across the room the panel leaves, as Starfall's crystals fell from over the camera, unseen), reported as the hero's own cast reports it (a bow's arrows taken from the quiver first, `Player.nockTime`: without, the ranger drew an empty string). A drag holds the camera along the row (`dragHall`, `dropHall`: the hero nearest in focus as it's let go). Its heroes are kept between openings, out of the scene while it's down (`kept`, `heroIn`), and built again only for other gear or detail; a class never played in the slot keeps the starter gear the hall last showed (`profileOf`: `loadProfile` rolls it afresh at every call, so each opening built two heroes again, in other gear). Built afresh at every opening, the three held the click that opened it up 190 to 243 ms, every time; kept, the opening's longest frame is 21 to 28 ms (`npm run stalls -- --scenario heroes --js`). Back rebuilt the lobby's dummies (their straw is now made once, `strawOnce` in `models/dummy.ts`): its longest frame 49 to 63 ms, now 35 to 42. A hero's glances round (`models/glance.ts`) wait until his showcase is done (`Player.glances`). A hero turns to his showcase's spot and back by a critically damped spring (`turnTo`: eased in a frame by the angle, he set off at full speed). Its heroes are posed beside the lobby's own, hidden but still posed: a model's state carried from frame to frame must be its own (the warriors shared their way back to the guard, and the hall's axe jumped 2.2 m in a frame). Closing it ends a showcase under way (`endShowcase`): left open, Void Lance's channel hummed on in the lobby. `npm run lab -- hall` follows the hero in focus through his showcase and after it, frame by frame. Its room and every hero are warmed with the `heroes` need (`heroParts`).
- `groundItems.ts`: the lobby floor. `biome.ts`: the lobby's biome choice (a cookie; Random rolls one each run). `stats.ts`: each player's run in numbers, for the scores.

## Gotchas

- **The lobby floor** (`game/groundItems.ts`): gear given up in the lobby (dragged onto the game field, Shift-click, the touch sheet's Drop) is thrown on the floor, not deleted. Clicking its name (drawn with the other overlays and picked on the canvas, `bindPicking`: the press never reaches the game; on touch a tap shows the card, a second tap takes it) puts it back in the stash, if the class can use its base and the stash has room. In co-op every game shows every drop and the host decides who got one first (`gdrop` / `gtake` / `gtaken` in `net/session.ts`). Whatever is left on the floor is lost when a run starts, and a guest leaving the room leaves it to the host. Every drop is drawn by three instanced meshes, one a piece (its shard, the glow under it and its shaft of light), each drop coloured by its rarity and each piece drawn in one pass: as meshes of their own a drop cost 8 draw calls (additive and double-sided, three.js draws each twice), and 120 drops added 10 to 14 ms to a frame.
- **How a wave comes in** (`startWave`, `spawnGroup` in `game/run.ts`): its foes come in groups, each group whole by one gate or split over two, three or four, at random, in packs of random sizes rising at their gates together (`packSizes`), and by the gates the wave has sent fewest packs by, at random among equals (`gatesFor`), so no gate stands idle for a wave once its packs are enough for all four (two gates picked at random a group, often one twice, left two or three idle; dealt round all four one foe a gate, every group came alike). A foe rises 0.5 to 3.5 m in from its gate and up to 3 m to the side of its middle, a spot drawn again while it is in a prop (`riseSpot`: risen inside one, a foe was shoved out of it in a frame as it first moved; by the Thornwood's west gate that was a log). The boss isn't in a group: it comes once the wave's first group has come, as that group would reach the players (`bossAt`: on average over its foes, each one's rise and then a straight walk at its wave's pace to within its reach of the nearest player), give or take `bossJitter`, never before that group is all up, or at once when the rest of the wave has all come and none of it stands; first in, a ranger could bring it down before the rest came. It comes by its gate's middle, 2 m further in (it's nearly as wide as the gate). The pace is the groups': one `groupInterval` after the last, or at once when none of the wave stands. Measure with `npm run lab -- spawns` against `main`.
