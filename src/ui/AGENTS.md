# `src/ui`

The page's UI over the game.

The rules across the game are the [root AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md); each directory under this one has an AGENTS.md of its own.

- **The lobby and menus:** `menus.ts` (the sanctuary with equipment and stash, the summary, pause and help), `loadoutEditor.ts`, `skillStrip.ts`, `attributes.ts`, `stashFilter.ts`, `tooltip.ts`, `skillDamage.ts` (a skill tooltip's damage), `itemIcons.ts` (an SVG icon per item base), `saves.ts` (the save slots screen), `heroSelect.ts` (the hero selection screen over the hall of heroes), `coop.ts` (the room dialog, the partners' frames, the downed and the revive), `scoreboard.ts`.
- **The run:** `hud.ts` (bars, hotbar, score, target frame, minimap, spoils, the choice after a wave), `floaters.ts` (damage numbers and labels over the world, drawn by `overlay/`), `damageMeter.ts` (the dummies' readout), `touch.ts` (the touch controls), `ping.ts` (co-op pings: Left Shift held and let go, a long press on the minimap).
- **The performance report prompt** (`#perf-ask`) is `src/perf/consent.ts`'s, its styles `styles/_perfAsk.scss`: never in a fight, under the choice after a wave while that shows, its buttons a finger's height on touch (`lab ui --states perfAsk,perfAskBreak,perfAskTouch`).
- **Around it:** `scale.ts` (the UI scaled as a share of the screen), `focus.ts` (a clicked control gives its focus back), `loading.ts` and `logoWind.ts` (the loading screen and its exit), `pwa.ts` (the install link and the reload offered on a new deploy), `perfHud.ts`, `netHud.ts` and `battleLog.ts` (the overlays down the left, each switched in the pause menu, the performance overlay in three sizes), `clipboard.ts` (their reports copied), `skillArt.ts` (the skill icons), `altCursor.ts` (the cursor drawn over mouse look while Alt is held).

The PWA's worker and caches are `scripts/AGENTS.md`; the skill icons' pictures `skillArt/AGENTS.md`; the world's overlays `overlay/AGENTS.md`; the stylesheets `styles/AGENTS.md`.

## Gotchas

- **The UI must fit any screen size, desktop and mobile.** Anything that overflows or overlaps at some viewport size is a bug.
  - Scale: `--ui` for the lobby and modals, `--hud` for the in-run HUD, `--touch` for the touch controls (all set in `src/ui/scale.ts`, which `uiScales` exports for code).
  - **The UI is a share of the screen, whatever the OS's scaling**: a 4K screen shows it the same at 100% as at 300% (the fit has no ceiling: capped at 1.35, the UI at 100% was half its size at 300%). Only small screens (phones, small windows) keep a floor in css px, so they stay readable, and the touch controls are sized for a finger in css px. Everything drawn over the screen follows it: the tooltips' cards (`zoom`), the world's labels (drawn at `--hud`, the floor's names at `--ui`: their art is in px of the reference layout, `Label.sizedBy`), and the loading screen, styled before any script runs (its `--u` in `index.html`, the same fit in CSS). An element scaled with `scale:` keeps its offsets from the screen's edges unscaled, so give them as `hud()` too; a `zoom`ed one's are zoomed with it. Check with `npm run lab -- ui --screens 3840x2160@1,1280x720@3`: the same lines, and pictures apart only by text's antialiasing (the lobby's text drifts up to 1% of the screen down a panel: Blink rounds each line of text to whole css px).
  - Layouts: on desktop the lobby is a CSS grid `[left | center loadout | right]` with the panels against the screen's edges; each folds away to its edge on its own, leaving a labelled tab (`FOLDS` in `ui/menus.ts`, kept in `localStorage`). A fold tab stands outside its panel's frame, so a panel that scrolls does it inside (the left one's `.ml-body`), never on the panel itself. Short screens (phones held sideways, height ≤ 520) and upright tablets show one panel at a time, picked from `#lobby-rail`, and the camera view shifts so the character stays beside the panel (`lobbyViewShift`); they never fold a panel. Phones held upright get a "turn your device" overlay.
  - Nothing moves as the class changes: text that differs by class is laid over the other classes' in one grid cell, only the current one shown, so its box is the largest's (the class card's name and tagline: the warrior's name pushed the class buttons onto a line of their own and the ranger's tagline took two lines, the panel growing by up to 40 px).
  - Check desktop at 1024×700 and 1920×1080, a phone at 844×390 and a tablet at 1180×820 and 820×1180 (Playwright with `isMobile` + `hasTouch`).
- **Co-op pings** (`ping.ts`, the pings themselves `game/ping.ts`, the message `src/net/AGENTS.md`): Left Shift held shows the ping letting go would make as a preview, faint and still, placed as the aim moves, on the ground or the foe under it (its ring and pin at `PREVIEW` strength, the foe outlined as Quarry Mark's aim does: `placePreview`), and a hint low in the middle (`.pinghint`) saying what it would ping; nothing is sent until it's let go, which pings where the preview stands, as Veilstep shows its landing while held (from play: "it needs to show ping preview on hold, and ping on release", "while I hold it should change the mark placement, ground or mob, then release to commit"). Every ping is one size, a foe's ring and pin centred on it as drawn (from play: "do not increase radius, just have same size for all... center point above mob. not side": a drag made an area and a foe's ring took its size; its pin stood by the copy's `pos`, off its body). The key was Ctrl, then G: Ctrl's keys are Chrome's too (Ctrl+W closes the tab, Ctrl+1 to 4 switch tabs, and a page can't stop them; from play, it "can change tab"). Each ping shows in the world as a ring laid on the floor (`floorPatch`, the effects' additive material: its program is warmed with them, its sample `pingSamples` in `game/warmup.ts`) with a ripple running out once every `PING.ripple` s (1.2: slower than the 1.5 a second the root allows, none with reduced motion) and a pin over it, a label of the overlay (`ORDER.ping`) naming who pinged and how far, held near the screen's edge with an arrow the way to it when it's off screen (`onScreenOrEdge` in `floaters.ts`, as a downed partner's marker is; `EDGE`, nearer the edge than the marker: in first person most pings are off screen and the arrow is the way to turn, from play "near the edge of screen is better"; its words under it at the top edge, and kept off the minimap, `offMinimap`, the minimap's box read twice a second, not every frame), in the pinger's hero colour; and on the minimap (`onMinimap` in `hud.ts`). On touch the minimap takes the finger (`body.touch #minimap`: on desktop it lets the mouse through to the game) and a finger held there (`PING.longPress`; a move past `pressSlop` before then cancels it) shows the ping, placed as it moves, made as it lifts, the browser's context menu kept off it. `npm run lab -- coop --scenarios ping` (`--pictures`: `out/coop/ping-*.png`).
- **Touch** (`src/ui/touch.ts`): touch mode (`body.touch`, `input.touchMode`) follows the last pointer used, and the browser's emulated mouse events are ignored in it.
  - Controls: a move stick on the left half and the skill buttons on the right; two fingers anywhere but the buttons pinch to zoom and twist to turn the camera, as the middle drag turns it with the mouse (`TWIST_START`: a pinch's own wobble turns nothing; a second finger down lets the stick go, its finger walking the hero no further), the world turning with the fingers (`npm run lab -- coop`). The stick's travel is its size as drawn (`--touch`), its knob under the finger, full speed from 0.6 of the travel and however far past its edge the finger goes (held to the edge and divided by the finger's whole distance, half a screen out walked the hero at a seventh of his speed; check with `npm run lab -- touch`, on a phone's `--screen` too). The basic attack, channels and drawn bows act while held. A skill aimed by holding (`SkillDef.aimed`) is held as its key is, its own preview showing where it would land, and cast as the finger lifts; the rest cast on release, a plain ring showing a drag's spot. A drag aims by hand, with the right thumb while the left moves; a tap aims at the nearest foe, but a movement skill's (Veilstep) goes as far as it reaches the way the stick points, or the way the hero faces with it let go (at the nearest foe, a tapped blink landed the mage among the foes). The buttons have no tooltip: a tap's click opened the skill's card pinned over them, and the next tap there landed on the card. Check with `npm run lab -- tap --class mage` (and `ranger`).
  - A control clicked or tapped gives its focus back (`ui/focus.ts`): left focused, a class button showed the browser's focus ring at the game's next key, clipped by its group's frame to a second border down its side, and Space or Enter pressed it again. Check with `npm run lab -- ui --states clicked`.
  - The touch HUD's top corners sit at the screen's edges, with no safe-area inset (`$touch-edge`, `$touch-beside` in `styles/_theme.scss`): a phone held sideways has its camera cutout in the middle of its short side, and the inset (34 px on the owner's S25 Edge) and a margin stood the pause button, the scores button and the bars 44 px in, where the minimap's corner never took one. What reaches the middle of a side (the skill buttons' arc) keeps it. In touch mode the choice after a wave stays clear of the bars, between them while that leaves it 460 px of the reference layout and under them otherwise (an upright tablet's), its buttons 32 css px tall at least (`_touch.scss`). On a short screen the choice after a wave stays in the top quarter, its spoils and risk on one line and its buttons as tall as anywhere (narrowed in touch mode alone: with the mouse's keys beside them, its labels wrapped), and the minimap has a thin line round it in place of its bezel (`_responsive.scss`). Check with `npm run lab -- ui --states hudTouch,decisionTouch --screen 832x384@2.8125 --insets 34,0,0,0`.
  - **Full screen is a button of its own** (`#btn-full`, `initFullScreen` in `touch.ts`), in the top right corner (its arrows out, in while the page is full screen, none where the browser can't go full screen: an iPhone's Safari), in a run beside the minimap, left of it (`--mm-room`, the minimap's width, `_hud.scss`); the corner's column down the left edge is the pause button and the scores under it, the overlays down the left (`#diag`) below them. A phone's browser bar takes a share of a short screen, and an installed app shows none. Check with `npm run lab -- ui --states hudTouch,logTouch,perfAskTouch --screen 832x384@2.8125 --insets 34,0,0,0`.
  - **Every touch button takes its taps wherever it shows** (the pause, full screen and scores buttons, the stowed lobby's Practice): nothing over it, even unseen. Stowed on a short screen, the lobby's rail is gone from sight but for Practice and lets every other tap through (`_responsive.scss`): it took the pause and full screen buttons' under it in the lobby's practice, and they did nothing on the owner's phone in a browser tab, which has no safe-area inset to push the rail clear of them. The stowed rail's Practice (Back) stands in the touch HUD's corner under the pause button, above the overlays down the left: half way down the rail the performance overlay lay over it, and a phone with it shown couldn't leave practice; stood aside for it, the overlays left the edge. Its offsets are the screen's divided by `--ui`, as its panel's zoom scales them too: as they were, it lay half over the pause button on a phone of a high ratio (751x298 at 3.75). Check with `npm run lab -- ui --states buttonsTouch,buttonsRunTouch` at each screen above, and a phone's with and without `--insets 34,0,0,0`: each button shown has its box clear of every other button's, is found at its middle and tapped by a finger, and what it's for must happen, with the overlays down the left shown and without (`--canary` lays a clear sheet over the pause button).
  - Everything hover-only needs a touch path. Tooltips open on tap (`bindTooltip`); items open an action sheet (`openItemSheet`); skills are bound by tapping a key and then the skill. Drag and drop and Shift are desktop extras.
  - **An item's sheet keeps its buttons in view** (`#item-sheet`, `_modals.scss`): its cards scroll inside it and the buttons stand under them, the box sized in the window's units over `--ui` (it's zoomed: its `86vh` was 86% of the window times the UI's scale, and the box scrolled the buttons below a phone's screen with two shields' long block notes, though there was room round them). On a short screen its cards take the room across it, in smaller text. Check with `npm run lab -- ui --states sheetTouch --screen 832x384@3.75` (and the screens above).
  - **A long press on an item lights its slot's items until the next tap** (`menus.ts` `pressAt`, `liftFrom`): on touch the light follows the finger's press, not hover, and stays as it lifts; a tap on a lit item keeps it (its sheet too), a tap anywhere else puts it out, and so does equipping. A tap shorter than the focus's delay lights nothing, and opens the item's sheet; a long press lights alone (`longPressClick`: the browser clicks after a long press too, and a stash item's sheet opened with the light; with the sheet skipped, the tap's tooltip stayed pinned in its place, so it's put away too). It went out as the finger lifted (the pointer's leave), and on Android a long press is also a drag's start (which put it out) and a context menu (which unequipped or equipped the item): neither does anything on touch. Check with `npm run lab -- ui --states litTouch` on a phone's screen.
- **The stash's slot focus waits for the pointer to rest** (`menus.ts` `focusSlot`, `FOCUS_DELAY_MS`: 200 ms): the item under the pointer is lit at once in full (its colours, its glow fading in, the glow's loops: `.focal`), the others that fit its slot light up (and the rest dim) once it has rested there, and they all go out the moment it leaves. Hovering fast held the owner's 144 Hz at 95 frames a second with every change of focus at once (`npm run stalls -- --scenario sweep --item-ms 100`), and the whole focus waiting (the item under the pointer too) looked laggy to the owner. A change of focus paints nothing (`_items.scss`): the others are lit by their glow (a layer painted once) and their size, the rest dimmed by their opacity, each at once. Lit and dimmed by their filters and colours too, every change painted the 60 items again, and fading in, the 30 to 60 fades were the page's to step every frame: resting on each item in turn (`--item-ms 300`) held 80 to 90 frames a second, 125 to 137 without. Read a change's cost in the stalls report's changes of the page's look (each kind's work over the quiet phase's).
- **The lobby's render reads no layout** (`renderMenu` in `menus.ts`): it rewrites the panels, and a size read in the middle of it lays the page out there, again after it. The skill strip's arrows are read in the next frame (`syncSkillStrip`), the stash chips' room kept by an observer (`chipsWidth` in `stashFilter.ts`): the two reads were 8.7 and 17.5 ms of the click that chose a first visit's hero (`npm run stalls -- --scenario boot`, the lobby's frames held up).
- **The tooltip follows the pointer by its transform alone** (`tooltip.ts` `place`, on a layer of its own): its size is measured when it's shown and when the window's size changes. Measured on every move, the comparison's stacked cards were taken apart and stacked again each time, two layouts of them, and placed by `left` and `top` it was laid out and painted again with what lay under it (10 to 16 ms of the browser's work after each move, in the stalls report).
- **A stowed lobby's panels take nothing** (`toggleMenuStowed` in `menus.ts`, `#menu.stowed .panel *` in `styles/_lobby.scss`): faded out for practice, they're `inert` and take no pointer anywhere in them. A `.btn` takes the pointer by its own rule, which won over its panel's `pointer-events: none`, so Enter the Arena, unseen, started a fight when clicked where it lies (from play: "arena fight can be started by clicking on that button while that pane is hidden"). The rail's Practice, a short screen's way back, keeps its own rule. - **The lobby's practice keeps the hotbar it built** (`hud.ts` `buildHotbar`): it's built again only for another hero or other skills in its slots. Space stows the lobby into practice and back, and built on every press, each slot was laid out and its icon painted afresh: 34 to 41 ms of the browser's work after each (`npm run stalls -- --scenario stow`).
- **The overlays down the left move by the compositor** (`#diag`'s `translate`, `menus.ts` `clearOverlays`), kept clear of the lobby's panels every frame while one is shown, and in a run under the party frames (`partyClearance`, their box read again only as the party, the window, the HUD's look or touch mode changes, or the frames' own size: the mini performance overlay lay over a partner's frame, #492). Placed by `left`, every move laid them out and painted them again, and the fold tab's box read every frame forced a layout as the panels slid (Space pressed rapidly kept them sliding). Their clearance is read from the page until the panels rest in a way they're laid out (`restKey`: the window, a short screen's panel, the hero panel folded), then remembered, and forgotten when a panel's size changes.
- **Digits are lining** (`font-variant-numeric: lining-nums` on the page, `_base.scss`): the body face's own are old-style, rising and dropping along a row of numbers as lower-case letters do ("digits are all jumping up and down in the same line"). The property replaces, not adds: a rule that sets it keeps `lining-nums` (`lining-nums tabular-nums`), and the controls, whose browser style sets their font afresh, inherit it. The world's labels paint in the body face's lining twin (`overlay/AGENTS.md`). `npm run lab -- ui` checks every state: each look of the page's text showing digits, set out alone and pictured, and each font of the labels, with their digits' tops and bottoms on a line.
- **A skill's tooltip states the damage the hero deals with it now** (`skillDamage.ts`, from the skill's `hits` in `combat/skills`): each kind of hit's range (the roll's ±10%, `DAMAGE_ROLL`), a second's for a channel's ticks and how often they come, what the figure is made of (the base, the percentages its tags take, `damageScale` as `rollPlayerDamage` takes it) and the crit; a skill that heals or wards the hero, what it gives him now. It's built each time it opens, from the hero's skill as his weapons have it (`Player.known`) and his stats, so it follows gear and attributes; another save's skills show their base figures (`'base'`), and the touch buttons have none. Check with `npm run lab -- tooltips` (every hit a skill lands within what its tooltip states, and the figures following a weapon's damage) and `npm run lab -- ui --states skillTips,skillTipsTouch` at each screen above (every skill's tooltip within the window, opened by the mouse over the hotbar and by a tap in the lobby).
- **The cursor is the game's own**, from the loading screen on: a gilded arrow, lit with an ember's glow over what takes a click. Check with `npm run lab -- ui`: every state's `cursors` line, the system's over any element is a problem.
- **The cursor over mouse look** (`altCursor.ts`): the close views hold the pointer locked, so the browser shows no cursor and sends every click to the canvas, and the HUD's buttons (the battle log's Pause, Copy, Clear) couldn't be pressed.
