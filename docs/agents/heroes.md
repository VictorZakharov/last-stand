# The heroes' gear and moves

Part of [`src/entities/models/AGENTS.md`](../../src/entities/models/AGENTS.md), kept here as only work on a hero's model needs it: the gear an item shows, the hands and how they hold, the ranger's look against his reference sheet, armour and the skirts' drape (`models/armor.ts`), the engraving, and the keyed full-body moves, the draught and the idles (`models/motion.ts`). The head is in [heads.md](heads.md), the ranger's bow in [ranger-bow.md](ranger-bow.md) and the legs in [legs.md](legs.md). Read it before changing any of them, and add what you learn to it.

the mage's whole robe, its cowl, bell sleeves, skirts and panels with the pauldrons, collar flaps and brooch (the panels skinned to bend halfway down, each half swung out at least as far as the draped robe under it: in one piece, over a knee raised at a run the panel hung behind the robe or stood straight out), over a plain linen tunic, breeches and boots (his free hand hangs as a relaxed hand does, palm to the thigh and thumb forward, whenever no spell poses it: turned palm back, its width stood across the body and its thumb's side went into the hip and the skirt; and the free arm swings out just enough to keep the hand and the forearm's cuff off the thigh and the cloth over it, `clearThigh`, as a hand brushing a moving thigh rests on the skirt) (the tunic shaped over the shoulders as a man's are, the trapezius sloping from the neck and a deltoid over each joint with the sleeve's top sunk in it: a round body with the arms' tubes beside it read as a coat hanger; facelab's `shoulders.ts`; `models/tunic.ts`, the ranger's coat cut on it too, with a standing collar: a coat ending in a shelf at the shoulders left a long bare neck from behind)), the bracers and gloves with a hands item (`Gear.hands`: without them the sleeves run on to the wrists and the hands are bare, `bare` in `models/hands.ts`, in `handSkin`: the ears' and neck's skin a shade darker, as plain skin beside the face's shaded paint reads pale). Armour that comes off is built on meshes of its own (a `Sculpt` of its own), never merged with what stays.
- `models/hands.ts`: three-jointed fingers. A hand holding something stays on its wrist: it turns so the handle runs across the palm (its length along the forearm, the wrist bending only as the handle needs), and the weapon is seated in that palm (`seat`, a grip joint moved to where the fist closes, whenever its turn changes). `hold()` then closes each finger snugly round the handle (each segment bends just until its end touches it, whatever its radius), the thumb over them from the other side. A handle may run diagonally across the palm (`slant`, up to `SLANT`: as a real hand can hold it, which spares the wrist that much of its bend; each finger closes where the slanted handle crosses it, and `seat` seats the weapon in that fist), the slant the arm IK chose. A hand reaching a grip it doesn't carry (a two-hander's lower grip) puts its wrist where its fist lands on it (`fistReach`) and reaches that by arm IK, turning about the handle as far as its arm needs (`roll`), and lets go of a grip no turn makes possible.
- **The hands are sculpted** (`models/handShape.ts`), one surface skinned to their joints, and looked at with `npm run lab -- hands` (each pose from each side, close-ups of the glove's cuffs and the thumb's web, the size against a man's: length 10.9% of his height, breadth 46% of the length, thickness 38% of the breadth). The shape is a distance field on the hand's own joints (`models/sdf.ts`: tubes, ellipsoids, tapered slabs and half-spaces, smoothly joined or cut) meshed by surface nets (`models/surfaceNets.ts`): a palm domed on its back and cupped in its middle, the thenar and the little finger's mound, a knuckle over each finger and a ridge of pads under them, the webs, each segment of each digit a tapering, flattened tube with a pulp at its tip. Each vertex follows the joints whose parts it lies on (its say falling off with the distance, `FALLOFF`), so a finger bends as skin does. What went wrong on the way: built of a rounded box and a tube a segment, the hand was a pillow with rows of sausages on it; with a ball at each joint, the fingers were strings of beads (the joints' parts now give weights alone, `sayOnly`), and segments joined softly swelled into rings at each joint (`SEGMENT_BLEND` is 1.2 mm).
  - The glove is the same field 1.4 mm further out, cut off at each finger's cuff (0.55 of its first segment, the thumb's at half its second) with a rolled hem, and under it only the skin it leaves bare is drawn (`tips`, on the skin's own vertices): the skin whole under it showed through wherever the bent thumb pressed the two together. A cuff cuts only near its own digit (`CUFF_REACH`: at 1.9 of its width the thumb's cut a hole by the index's root). The nails are plates laid on the fingertips' backs, found by stepping along to the skin (`surfaceAlong`: bisected between the search's ends, they missed every fingertip).
  - Each side is sculpted once, by a worker of its own as the game loads (`preloadHands`, `hands.worker.ts`; `main.ts` waits for them after summoning the hero), about 230 ms; on the page the two held the boot up (12.3 s against 5.8). Every hand of a side shares its geometry (`userData.shared`), and `lab hands` reports a side sculpted on the page as a problem (`SCULPTED_BY`).
  - The hands' materials are copies of their own, skinned by dual quaternions (`models/dualQuaternion.ts`): `hold` turns the thumb's root half round its length to close it over a handle, nail outward as a real one is, and three's own skinning, which blends matrices, pulled every vertex the thumb shares with the palm through its axis, folding the glove into a fin across the web. Their maps are read triplanar on the vertices' sculpted places (`models/triplanar.ts`), as the sculpt has no texture places: read flat along the axis each vertex faced most, the leather streaked down the hand's sides.
  - Main's hand of pieces was 239 mm long, 36% as broad and 55% as thick, 4,844 triangles in 18 meshes; the sculpt is 241 mm, 44% and 40% (gloved), 13,615 triangles in 3 meshes gloved and 12,184 in 2 bare.

- **Hero moves are full-body and keyed** (`models/motion.ts`): an attack is written as keyframed curves over its progress (`curve(keys, k)`, a monotone cubic: an overshoot or a hold is a key of its own), one per channel, and moves the whole body, not just the arms: a wind-up (the weight back onto the rear foot, the hips and chest coiling away), the blow led from the ground up (the hips turn first, the spine and chest after, the arm and wrist last: the same curve read a few hundredths earlier for each joint down the chain), the weight forward and down into it, a follow-through past the hit, the other arm countering and the head kept on the foe. Keep a move's hit where the skill fires (the swing's tip crosses the arc from 0.55 to 0.85 of the cast, in step with the trail). `PoseFade` crossfades every cut between poses (an action starting, restarting or ending: from the pose shown just before, over a fifth of a second or so, slower out of a blow that should be seen to recover), so a move may end on its follow-through and let the fade bring it home. A short cast's end (a throw, a blow, a roar) is held a moment after the cast is over and then eased out (`TAIL` / `TAIL_OUT` in each hero model; moving off or another action cuts it), so it is seen without slowing the gameplay.
- **A blow into the ground** (the warrior's `smite`, Thunder Crescent's): the two-hander is raised up and back over the head as the weight goes back, held a moment at the top, and driven down before him at the skill's `fireAt` (0.55 of the cast), the trunk crunching over it and the knees giving. Three things put the weapon on the floor, whatever its length and head:
  - the blade turns in the upright plane straight ahead all through the move (`aimAtFloor`, before the arm is fitted within its ranges), up and back over the head as it's raised (`SM_BLADE`), then falling, faster as it comes, until it points at the floor as far off as its length reaches;
  - from just before it lands the hands come to rest together before his middle, 0.6 m ahead of his feet and 0.8 m up (`holdAtFloor`, `holdInWorld` as the run's high port places a two-hander);
  - the floor stops it, and once landed it rests there (`groundStop`: the least turn of the arm about its shoulder that leaves no point of the weapon under the floor, every corner of its surface, `lowPoints`): a greatsword rests on its tip, an axe on its bit and a maul on its face. Stopped by its tip alone, the greataxe's head went 17.6 cm into the floor and the maul's 21.3; each point lifted by a turn of its own, the haft's end behind the hands was pulled down to the floor as the blow landed and the lower hand let go; and points too near the shoulder to reach the floor, counted as turned at most straight down, let the maul's butt below the shoulder hold the blow a metre up. `lab range` reads the weapon under the floor;
  - a cut out of it fades from the arms as drawn (`PoseFade.drawnArms`), as the steps above turn the weapon and stop it after the fade: faded from the pose before them, it was back at rest in a frame as the end's hold was over, its tip 1.3 m away. Every other cut fades from the pose's own: from the arms as fitted, the cut into a two-handed swing blended two elbows and the lower grip went 9 cm out of the left arm's reach. The end's hold is over on the frame its cut fades from it: held a frame longer, the steps turned and stopped the weapon again over the arms they had drawn, and the arm's solve (which looks for the elbow round last frame's) swung the elbow round 30° in two frames, the greatsword's tip 26 cm a frame.
