# The heroes' gear and moves

Part of the gotchas in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), kept here as only work on a hero's model needs it: the gear an item shows, the hands and how they hold, the ranger's look against his reference sheet, armour and the skirts' drape (`models/armor.ts`), the engraving, and the keyed full-body moves, the draught and the idles (`models/motion.ts`). The head is in [heads.md](heads.md), the ranger's bow in [ranger-bow.md](ranger-bow.md) and the legs in [legs.md](legs.md). Read it before changing any of them, and add what you learn to it.

- `Head.hair` lists the hair's meshes, which a model hides under a helmet: a hero wears its helmet or hat only with a head item equipped (`Gear.helm`), bareheaded otherwise, and the rest of its armour likewise: the body's with a chest item (`Gear.chest`: the warrior's breastplate, pauldrons, mail skirt, tassets and knee cops over his gambeson, trousers and boots (the mail draped over his trousers, the boots' fur cuffs and his hands as the mage's skirts are: swung alone, it was narrower than the trousers at its sides and they came through it in every frame; its top gathered under the waist belt against the leather there: hung below the belt and wider than it, the ring between the two was open and the trousers' tops showed through it at the sides; and his left arm, when that hand holds no weapon, empty or on the shield's grip, swung out off the thigh as the mage's free arm is); the mage's whole robe, its cowl, bell sleeves, skirts and panels with the pauldrons, collar flaps and brooch (the panels skinned to bend halfway down, each half swung out at least as far as the draped robe under it: in one piece, over a knee raised at a run the panel hung behind the robe or stood straight out), over a plain linen tunic, breeches and boots (his free hand hangs as a relaxed hand does, palm to the thigh and thumb forward, whenever no spell poses it: turned palm back, its width stood across the body and its thumb's side went into the hip and the skirt; and the free arm swings out just enough to keep the hand and the forearm's cuff off the thigh and the cloth over it, `clearThigh`, as a hand brushing a moving thigh rests on the skirt) (the tunic shaped over the shoulders as a man's are, the trapezius sloping from the neck and a deltoid over each joint with the sleeve's top sunk in it: a round body with the arms' tubes beside it read as a coat hanger; facelab's `shoulders.ts`; `models/tunic.ts`, the ranger's coat cut on it too, with a standing collar: a coat ending in a shelf at the shoulders left a long bare neck from behind)), the bracers and gloves with a hands item (`Gear.hands`: without them the sleeves run on to the wrists and the hands are bare, `bare` in `models/hands.ts`, in `handSkin`: the ears' and neck's skin a shade darker, as plain skin beside the face's shaded paint reads pale). Armour that comes off is built on meshes of its own (a `Sculpt` of its own), never merged with what stays.
- `models/hands.ts`: three-jointed fingers. A hand holding something stays on its wrist: it turns so the handle runs across the palm (its length along the forearm, the wrist bending only as the handle needs), and the weapon is seated in that palm (`seat`, a grip joint moved to where the fist closes, whenever its turn changes). `hold()` then closes each finger snugly round the handle (each segment bends just until its end touches it, whatever its radius), the thumb over them from the other side. A handle may run diagonally across the palm (`slant`, up to `SLANT`: as a real hand can hold it, which spares the wrist that much of its bend; each finger closes where the slanted handle crosses it, and `seat` seats the weapon in that fist), the slant the arm IK chose. A hand reaching a grip it doesn't carry (a two-hander's lower grip) puts its wrist where its fist lands on it (`fistReach`) and reaches that by arm IK, turning about the handle as far as its arm needs (`roll`), and lets go of a grip no turn makes possible.
- **The ranger's look** follows his reference sheet (a T-pose from the front, the side and the back, and his head from the front, three-quarters and the side), measured against it in a studio render framed as each panel is: each part split out by colour on both (ours by an id pass, each part a flat colour), where it starts and ends up the figure per the shoulders' height (not the figure's: the sheet's bow stands above the hat, and its arrows beside the head are as wide as the brim), its width there and its median colour (the skin's matching says the light does). The felt hat (`buildHat`, in the face's mm from the eyes: a crown rising from a leather band, near upright and then a dome, a wide brim turned down a little, the hair left on under it), the coat with a standing collar and a buttoned slit, its sleeves set into armholes cut in it as a tailor's are (`tunicCut`, `setInSleeve` in `models/tunic.ts`: the coat laid out from each armhole's edge and the sleeve's top that same edge, point for point, the sleeve leaving it along the coat's own surface all round, the armpit's fold turned in its first few centimetres; the cloth round the seam blended from the chest to the arm through joints turned a quarter, half and three quarters of the arm's swing, `delts`, the seam half: a sleeve's tube stood in a whole coat rose out of it as a tube stuck into the body when the arm went up for the quiver, the coat skinned straight from the chest to the arm folded in on the joint in a lip round the sleeve, a grid's points pushed out onto the hole's edge folded its triangles over in a jagged seam with gaps, and a sleeve turned back on the coat under the arm kept that crease raised: the seam moves as the cloth on both sides of it does, so the angle there is the rest pose's in every pose; measure that angle round the seam between the two shading normals, and look with the cloth flat-coloured by the joint it hangs on, its back faces in another colour and a checker in its uvs), its seams sewn in a lighter thread (`stitches` in `models/armor.ts`: dashes along a curve), belted high under a broad stitched belt, its skirt hung from under the belt and falling straight to mid-thigh, slit at the sides (hung from the hips it narrowed to a waist under the belt and flared out like a bell, half as deep again from the side), draped over the trousers and the boots and behind the hands, the cuffs and the bow's string and lower limb (`Skirt.update`'s bodies, the skirt last in the frame, once the arms and the bow are placed: swung by the thighs alone, the carried bow's string went through its front over the stepping thigh, and the nock into it whenever the bow waited before the body for the next arrow), wool trousers into tall riding boots (`models/boot.ts`: the foot sculpted over a last, a welted sole and heel on the floor the leg IK keeps the ankle over, the shaft skinned across the ankle and creased where it bends, a thick turned cuff, straps buckled on the outer side), a sleeve running into a bracer with a brass band round its top, tapering down the forearm to the wrist's flattened oval (`ovalTube`: round and as wide at the wrist as below the elbow, 30 cm round where a wrist is 17, it stood out from the hand as a pipe), and under its end a leather cuff over the wrist onto the heel of the hand, skinned through joints turned a quarter, half and three quarters as far as the hand (`wrists`, set with the shoulders' in `bendShoulders`, each a share of the hand's turn taken the way round nearer the middle of the turns that hand takes, `blendTurn`: taken the shorter way, the bow hand's turn near half round went one way and the other in turn, and through the eyes the cuff flickered between two shapes with the hand still; the middle's way round matters too, given the long way the cuff pinched in under the bracer) over 6 cm, so it bends with the hand (blended straight from the forearm to the hand over 3.5 cm, it kinked and its inside collapsed; the bracer a rigid tube to the wrist and the palm a box from it, a bent wrist swung the palm out of the tube's end; pinched in under the bracer's end, it read as a doll's joint), a long banded quiver on a baldric over the right shoulder and round the back.
- Its cloth and leather have their own maps (`wool`, `felt`, `oiled` in `core/pbr.ts`: a heathered plain weave, matted felt, smooth oiled leather with long creases and a sheen): the twill read as synthetic and the pebbled hide as snakeskin. Look at each region in a close-up beside the sheet's (the boots, the torso, the head), not only the whole figure.
- His face is his own (`FACES.ranger`, `LOOKS.ranger`): fitted with the face harness to the sheet's head views (with `--helm`, as the sheet wears the hat; its strict profile has no landmarks, so its 3D rebuild is run once by hand with a box round the face, `recon.py ... side=<picture>:x0,y0,x1,y1`, and the turnaround skips the side), from the mage's mask, with the warrior's lids and look (the mage's plain look left the face flat and the eyes staring) and a short full beard. Under the hat the hair's locks are hidden and its cap kept (`Head.hair`, the cap first): the locks hung out under the brim in strands. Below the hat's band his hair is short (`buildHead`'s `crop`, `Head.crop`: tufts of cards over the back and sides, lying down the head to a nape `nape` mm lower, kept on the skin by the head's distance field, their ends breaking the hairline), and the collar stands higher at the back (`COLLAR_RISE`): the cap alone was a flat black shell with a hard edge under the brim, and 16 cm of bare skin showed from it to a level collar. Measure from behind (`NECKRAYS` in the bow film, `neckback.py`: rays through the pixels round the neck classed by what they meet, the bare skin between the hair and the collar per column, and the skin's brightness across the neck row by row), standing and at full draw, as the turned head shows the side of the neck. The brim must keep clear of the draw arm: measure with rays along each arm's upper arm, forearm and hand as capsules against the hat's meshes through every shot (at the sheet's brim, turned down at the sides and back, the draw forearm ran under it through the whole full draw, and the hand went through the crown on its way to the quiver).
- `models/armor.ts`: plates with thickness (`plate()` faces them away from their joint), straps, fur, and `Skirt`, a continuous skirt that swings with the legs. Given the limbs (`update`'s `legs` and `hands`, `SkirtBody` capsules in the skirt's own space: the thighs from the hip joint and the shins over the boots, sized to what is actually on them (the boot's cuff and stone stand 10 cm off the shin) and grown by the cloth's gap, and each hand as capsules from its own joints: its palm's edges, index, middle and little fingers, thumb, and the free forearm's cuff), it is draped over them after its swing: each point inside a leg moved away from the skirt's middle and up until it is clear (put out by the nearest side, the points round a knee went every way round it and the cloth crumpled), each point a hand reaches or lies just outside within the points' spacing (`HAND_REACH`) put in behind it, the cloth between each point and the one above it and its neighbour round the row kept out of the legs too (`over`: where that line passes through a leg, the point on the far side, under a raised thigh, in front of a raised knee, of two neighbours the one nearer the skirt's middle, is put round onto the leg where the line went deepest and on over it by the length left; points outside a leg were left where they were, and over a thigh raised near level the front's middle split round it, one column over it and the next under; put only where the line went deepest, the cloth came up short over the thigh), every point held to its length down the cloth and round it (across the seam at the front middle, the last column but one tied to the first: tied to the copy of it, the cloth parted down the front and a stride's shin came out through the slit), ending off the legs (`layer`: an outer skirt lies that much further out). Swung by the thighs' angles alone, a knee raised at a run came through the front; swinging whole columns out past the knee stood the cloth out as a stiff cone. A model places its limbs with `SkirtLimbs` (capsules between points on its joints; `handCapsules` in `models/hands.ts` gives a hand's), and keeps a free arm's hand off the thigh with `armOffThigh`. Limbs are culled by their boxes, in runs whose boxes overlap (a hand's dozen capsules), and the lines are kept out of the legs in the last passes only: the robe's two skirts drape in about 0.6 ms a frame, the tunic's in 0.2 to 0.3, the warrior's mail in 0.3 to 0.4 and the ranger's coat, the finest of them, in about 0.6 to 0.7 (most of it the legs: some 5,500 points put out of a limb and 1,800 lines kept out of a leg a frame, so culling by boxes can't take much more off; 0.9 to 1.1 before its normals were made over the flat arrays, `vertexNormals`, to the bit as three's, and its loop over the points read each point's box before anything else, the cloth coming out the same; measure a drape's cost with `timeCalls` on `Skirt.prototype` in the lab, both sides in one A/B). Through the eyes the ranger's coat and the warrior's mail swing undraped (the body is on a layer neither the camera nor the lights draw: 0.04 ms against 0.35); the mage's skirts are still draped there, as his visible sleeves are pushed out of them (`Skirt.pushOut`). Measure clipping as surfaces crossing (the lines where a limb's triangles cross a skirt's, per frame, and which columns and heights), not by an envelope round the body, and look with the limbs painted flat red: the navy breeches through a navy robe are invisible in a render. Its `taperTube` / `twist` face outwards, unlike the creature versions in `shapes.ts`, which wind inwards.
- `core/engraving.ts`: the engraved steel atlas (breastplate, medallions, knee cops) and embroidered panels; the new tiling maps (leather, mail, cloth, steel, fur) are PBR recipes.
- **Hero moves are full-body and keyed** (`models/motion.ts`): an attack is written as keyframed curves over its progress (`curve(keys, k)`, a monotone cubic: an overshoot or a hold is a key of its own), one per channel, and moves the whole body, not just the arms: a wind-up (the weight back onto the rear foot, the hips and chest coiling away), the blow led from the ground up (the hips turn first, the spine and chest after, the arm and wrist last: the same curve read a few hundredths earlier for each joint down the chain), the weight forward and down into it, a follow-through past the hit, the other arm countering and the head kept on the foe. Keep a move's hit where the skill fires (the swing's tip crosses the arc from 0.55 to 0.85 of the cast, in step with the trail). `PoseFade` crossfades every cut between poses (an action starting, restarting or ending: from the pose shown just before, over a fifth of a second or so, slower out of a blow that should be seen to recover), so a move may end on its follow-through and let the fade bring it home. A short cast's end (a throw, a blow, a roar) is held a moment after the cast is over and then eased out (`TAIL` / `TAIL_OUT` in each hero model; moving off or another action cuts it), so it is seen without slowing the gameplay.
- The healing draught is drunk (`models/flask.ts`: the free hand takes a flask from the belt and arm IK lifts it to a mouth point on the head, from where each model holds it, `DrinkHold`; the warrior's shield hangs from that forearm before the chest meanwhile, a second weapon is worn on the belt (`DRINK_SHEATHED`: in a leather frog at the left hip, a sword by its guard with the blade raked back along the thigh, an axe or mace head up; `Weapon.hang`) and drawn again, a two-hander lets go of its lower grip, and the mage's sleeve is pushed back up the arm). Its beats are smooth keyed curves (`HIP`, `LIFT`, `TIP`, `OUT`), the flask aimed from the planned wrist, not the moving one (check with a per-frame speed and turn-rate probe). It is a `freeMove` cast: no other skill while it lasts, but full speed and facing the way the hero goes. A dash may pace itself (`DashOpts.pace`: Bull Rush braces, accelerates and skids, covering the same distance).
- The idles are alive: a stance with the weight shifting from foot to foot (the pelvis swaying over them, the hips tipping, the shoulders answering), breathing in the shoulders, the weapons at the ready, a blade held edge on to the foe in a neutral grip, thumb up and palm in, the wrist nearly straight (`REST_ARM`, `REST_TWO`: the forearm angled down so the blade rises from the fist; measure the wrist's bend from the forearm in the hand's frame) (`MOUNT`: in third person every weapon sits in its fist as a real one does, its flats along the palm and its edge or bit out over the knuckles; a move that must lead with the edge rolls the forearm, `edgeOn` about a blade along the arm: palm up on a forehand swing, down on a backhand, `SW_ROLL`, `FANG_ROLL`; never turn a blade held across the forearm that way, it bends the wrist instead. Check with a probe of the flat's normal against the tip's motion and the palm's against the flat). First person keeps its own hand-placed poses (`FP_*`); the full-body versions are third person only. Check a move with a frame-exact film (the game paused, `animate` stepped through the action by hand) from the side and the front.
