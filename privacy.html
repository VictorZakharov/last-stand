Privacy

Last Stand, an open-source browser game. Updated 10 November 2026.

Playing without an account

Your heroes, settings and save slots stay in this browser (its local storage and cookies). Nothing about them is sent anywhere. Clearing the site's data deletes them.

Cloud saves (optional)

If you sign in, the server keeps:

  • an account id the server makes up at random;
  • your passkeys' public keys (a passkey's private part never leaves your device);
  • the save of each site you sync (the game, and any test build you sign in on): your heroes, their gear, stash and records, skill loadouts, stash filters, your co-op name tag and colour, your volumes and portraits, which device last wrote each part, and when;
  • the sessions you're signed in with (only a fingerprint of each, never the session itself).

It keeps no name, email address, picture or password. Signing in with Google, when it comes, keeps only Google's id for your account, never your name or email.

The server is the game's own, run by the game's maintainer. Nothing is sold or shared, and nothing is used for advertising.

How long, and deleting it

  • Delete cloud save (Save slots, Account) deletes the current site's save. Your heroes stay on your device.
  • Delete account (on last-stand.cc) deletes your account, every save of it and its passkeys at once.
  • A test build's saves are deleted when the test build closes, or 30 days after their last sync.
  • Sessions you don't use for 180 days end.
  • Nightly backups of the server keep what was deleted for at most 14 more days.

Logs and reports

The server's logs name an account by the first letters of its id and never hold a save's contents. Performance reports are sent only when you agree to it in the game, and hold no account.

Questions

Ask on the game's issue tracker. You can ask for what the server keeps about you, or for it to be deleted.
